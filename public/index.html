About MY USEFUL TOOLS

Welcome to MY USEFUL TOOLS. We are a free, independent web portal dedicated to providing simple, fast, and accessible browser-based online calculators and digital utilities for students, professionals, shoppers, and everyday users worldwide.

Our Purpose

Our mission is to take the hassle out of common everyday numbers. Whether you need to find your exact chronological age, calculate monthly loan EMIs, solve percentage problems, figure out shopping sale discounts with sales tax, or check Body Mass Index (BMI), MY USEFUL TOOLS provides immediate answers without bloat or complications.

Core Principles of Our Platform

  • 100% Free & Open: Every calculator on our website is entirely free to use. We do not charge fees, offer paid tiers, or lock features behind paywalls.
  • Zero Mandatory Registration: You do not need to create an account, register your email, or log in. Every utility is ready to use the moment you open the page.
  • Client-Side Browser Processing: All calculations are executed directly within your web browser. Your numbers and personal dates stay on your device.
  • Fast & Mobile-First: Crafted with a lightweight, responsive interface that loads instantly and works smoothly on smartphones, tablets, laptops, and desktop computers.

Our Calculator Suite

MY USEFUL TOOLS features five focused, high-precision tools:

  • Age Calculator: Computes your exact age in years, months, and days, along with a countdown to your next birthday and total days lived.
  • EMI Loan Calculator: Calculates monthly installments, total interest, and total repayment schedules in INR (₹) or USD ($) for home, personal, and auto loans.
  • Percentage Calculator: Provides three essential solvers: finding the percentage of a number, calculating percentage increases or decreases, and determining proportions.
  • Discount Calculator: Determines discounted sale prices, total money saved, and optional sales tax in INR (₹) or USD ($).
  • BMI Health Calculator: Evaluates Body Mass Index using metric or imperial measurements, complete with World Health Organization categories and healthy weight ranges.

Our Commitment

We strive to keep our formulas aligned with standard mathematics and widely recognized guidelines. We continuously test our utilities across browsers to ensure responsive, consistent, and dependable results.
